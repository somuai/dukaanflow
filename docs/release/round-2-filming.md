# Round 2 demo recording

Aim for 2 minutes 40 seconds. Use the signed DukaanFlow build on the connected iPhone. Ask the kirana owner for permission to record their image and voice and to include the footage in the chosen public or unlisted YouTube submission. Use a separate shop profile with fictional records. Keep customers, invoices, notifications, and account details out of the frame.

Before filming, open DukaanFlow Home, select the Wispr Flow keyboard, and test a short dictation. Check that Flow is signed in, has microphone permission and Full Access, and has internet access. Record the screen and use a second camera for the owner's voice and hands. Rehearse both together with the screen recording microphone off. If that interferes with dictation, use the second camera to film the phone and capture the audio in one take. [Wispr setup](https://docs.wisprflow.ai/articles/7453988911-set-up-the-flow-keyboard-on-iphone) · [Apple screen recording](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102653)

| Time | Shot | What the reviewer should see |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:20 | Owner at the counter | One genuine sentence in their own words about tracking deliveries or credit. Identify the demonstration records as fictional. |
| 0:20–1:15 | Home note on iPhone | Tap the Wispr mic, speak, read the inserted transcript, correct it if needed, and tap Submit. Keep this sequence continuous. |
| 1:15–2:20 | Supplier, customer, and product screens | Confirm the delivery, the ₹100 credit and four-day follow-up, and the updated stock. |
| 2:20–2:40 | Reviewer entry point | Show the public repository and its one-command Docker instructions once published. |

Seed supplier **राज** and customer **राजू**. Add **आटा** and **दूध** in kg, **Bread** and **अंडा** in pieces, and **Butter** in packets. Start bread at 7, eggs at 3, and butter at 2. Start flour and milk at zero.

> राज मेरे supplier है माल का। आज उसने मेरे को 10 kg आटा और 5 kg दूध भेजा है। राजू ने मेरे से ₹100 उधार लिया है और उसके बदले 4 bread, 2 अंडा, 1 bread और 1 butter लिया है, 4 दिन में चुका देगा।

Expected result is a delivery of 10 kg flour and 5 kg milk, a ₹100 customer balance with a follow-up in four days, and 2 bread, 1 egg, and 1 butter remaining. This sentence has passed transcript-based testing. Record the live version before describing it as verified speech.

For a shorter take, start a fresh demo shop with zero flour and use “राज ने आज 10 किलो आटा भेजा और राजू ने मुझसे 2 किलो आटा खरीदा।” Stock should finish at 8 kg. If you use typing as a fallback, label it on screen. Upload the final clip as public or unlisted and verify it plays without signing in.
